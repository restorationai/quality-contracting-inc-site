## Why Southborough Renovation Projects Need a Specialized Final Clean

Southborough's housing stock is a mix of older farmhouse-style homes being renovated and larger new builds going up on bigger lots, and both come with their own cleaning challenges. Older homes being gutted or added onto often have plaster dust that behaves differently than drywall dust, it's finer, it clings to surfaces longer, and it tends to resettle if it isn't captured with the right vacuum filtration the first time. Newer construction on the town's larger parcels tends to generate more volume, simply because the square footage is bigger and there are more windows, more trim, and more fixtures to detail. Many properties here are also on private wells, which means mineral deposits from well water can leave spotting on new fixtures and glass that a standard cleaning pass won't fully remove.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

We work in stages rather than treating a final clean as one pass. The rough clean removes construction debris, stickers, labels, and bulk material so the space is safe to move through. The detail clean follows: wiping down every surface, cabinet interior and exterior, window sill, and baseboard to pull out the fine dust that drywall and sanding leave behind. Windows get cleaned inside and out, including tracks, where construction dust tends to compact into a paste if it's been sitting through a few humid days. HVAC vents and returns get wiped and, where needed, vacuumed, since a system that ran during construction has likely pulled dust through the ductwork. The final walk-through clean happens right before handoff, catching anything that settled after the detail pass, which is common on multi-week projects where trades are still finishing trim or touch-up paint.
