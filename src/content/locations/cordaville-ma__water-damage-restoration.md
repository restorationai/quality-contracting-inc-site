## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Cordaville

The first step on any call is finding where the water actually is, not just where it's visible. In homes with finished basements or additions built over older foundations, water often travels along joists and settles in spots well away from the original leak, so we use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent before pulling a single piece of flooring. Extraction comes next, using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how the property is accessed, followed by placement of air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space rather than a one-size-fits-all setup. Older plaster and lath walls, which still show up in a fair number of Cordaville's pre-1960s homes, hold moisture longer than drywall and need extended drying time even after surfaces read dry to the touch. We track moisture content daily and document readings for the insurance file, drying structural materials in line with IICRC S500 guidelines rather than guessing when a space is ready to close back up.
