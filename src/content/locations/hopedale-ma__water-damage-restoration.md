## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Hopedale

The first step is always extraction, pulling standing water off floors and out of carpet pad before it migrates further into subfloor and framing. In Hopedale's older homes, that often means working around plaster walls and real hardwood rather than drywall and engineered flooring, which changes both the equipment and the timeline. Plaster holds moisture and releases it slowly, so a wall that reads dry at the surface with a moisture meter can still be wet behind the lath. We track drying progress daily and follow IICRC S500 drying standards as a benchmark, adjusting dehumidifier and air mover placement as readings change rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. Once materials hit a stable, dry target, we document it before closing out the job so the paperwork matches what actually happened in the house.
