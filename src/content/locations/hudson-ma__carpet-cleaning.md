## Why Hudson Carpet Wears and Stains Differently

Hudson grew up as a shoe and mill town on the Assabet River, and a lot of that housing stock dates back to the late 1800s and early 1900s. Wood subfloors in these older homes shift seasonally with New England humidity, which means carpet laid over them can hold moisture longer after a cleaning than carpet over a modern slab. Add in central Massachusetts winters, where sand, rock salt, and roadway grime get tracked in from driveways and sidewalks for months at a time, and you get ground-in soil that sits deep in the pile rather than just on the surface. Spring thaw brings its own problem: rising humidity off the river valley can reactivate old stains that looked gone the winter before, especially tannin stains from boots and pet traffic.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Hudson

We start with a walk-through to identify traffic patterns, pet areas, and any pre-existing stains, which matters more in Hudson's older homes where carpet has often been laid directly over original hardwood or fir subflooring. Pre-treatment targets protein and tannin stains common in New England households, coffee, wine, pet accidents, before hot water extraction lifts embedded soil and salt residue out of the pile. For commercial spaces, including the small offices and retail units that have moved into converted mill buildings along the river, we adjust dwell time and extraction passes based on carpet tile versus broadloom and foot traffic volume. Every job finishes with grooming the pile and setting up fans or air movers so carpet dries evenly rather than wicking moisture back up from an older, slower-drying subfloor.
