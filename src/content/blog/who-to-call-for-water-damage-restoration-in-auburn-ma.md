## When to Call a Restoration Company vs. a Plumber

A restoration company and a plumber do different jobs, and knowing the difference saves you time and money.

**Call a plumber** when the source of water is still active and needs a physical repair: a burst pipe that needs replacing, a water heater that needs to be disconnected, or a supply line that needs a new fitting. Plumbers fix the source. They do not dry out your structure or remediate damage.

**Call a restoration company** once the water source is controlled (or simultaneously, if you're not sure). Restoration companies handle everything that happens after the water stops: extraction, structural drying, moisture mapping, mold prevention, contents protection, and rebuild. Quality Contracting handles all of these under one roof, which matters because the [IICRC S500 standard](https://www.iicrc.org) sets strict timelines for drying. Mold can begin developing within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. Every hour of delay matters.

Quality Contracting also works directly with your insurance company, documenting the loss with moisture readings, photo documentation, and a written scope of work. That documentation is what protects your claim. If you want more detail on what policies actually cover, see our guide: [Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Water Damage?](/blog/does-homeowners-insurance-cover-water-damage/)

**Call your insurance company** after mitigation has started, not before. Your insurer will ask what steps you've taken to prevent further damage. Having a restoration company already on-site shows you acted promptly, which supports your claim.

## What About Sewage Backup or Flooding?

Not all water damage is the same, and the category of water determines the safety protocols and cleanup approach.

- **Category 1 (clean water):** Supply line breaks, appliance failures, rainwater intrusion. Lowest health risk. Standard extraction and drying.
- **Category 2 (gray water):** Dishwasher or washing machine overflow, toilet overflow without solids. Moderate contamination. Requires antimicrobial treatment and more aggressive material removal.
- **Category 3 (black water):** Sewage backup, floodwater from outside, any water that has contacted the ground. High contamination. Full PPE for crew, aggressive removal of affected materials, antimicrobial treatment. Quality Contracting handles [sewage cleanup](/services/sewage-cleanup/) as a dedicated service.

If your basement flooded from outside during a heavy rain event, that water is Category 3 regardless of how it looks. Do not let anyone enter without proper PPE, and do not run HVAC systems that could spread contamination.
